Nursery & Innovation Hub

Mtwapa Nursery and Innovation Hub

Location: Mtwapa Agricultural Training Centre (ATC), Kilifi County

The Crescendo Green Nursery and Innovation Hub is one of Kenya’s leading centres for large-scale propagation of natural trees and high-value tree crops. Featuring a 7,500 m² shadehouse, the facility produces millions of high-quality seedlings annually for reforestation, agroforestry, and commercial plantations across East Africa.

Current focus species include cashew and moringa. Ongoing research covers improved planting material, advanced substrates, biochar, compost, biological soil amendments, and climate-resilient techniques. Technical partner: Growpact Global (Netherlands).

Flagship Restoration Project

Koromi Landscape Restoration and Agroforestry Project

Location: Near Baricho, Kilifi County • Size: 200 hectares

Crescendo Green’s flagship demonstration site for integrated landscape restoration and climate-smart land management in Kenya’s drylands. The project combines commercial forestry, regenerative agriculture, and ecosystem restoration.

Key species: Melia volkensii (improved varieties, planting densities, and soil amendment trials). Additional focus on agroforestry systems, rainwater harvesting, swales, carbon sequestration, and biodiversity enhancement under 600–700 mm annual rainfall.

Partners: JICA, KFS, KEFRI, Keystone Forestry Partners, Nature Kenya, ERC, and Capital Africa Agribusiness Ventures.

Experimental & Demonstration Site

Arabuko Farm

Location: Kakuyuni, near Arabuko-Sokoke ecosystem, Kilifi County • Size: 4 hectares

The original innovation hub of Crescendo Green. This experimental farm and demonstration nursery tested propagation techniques, water-efficient systems (drip irrigation), and cultivation of over 60 different tree species, including drought-tolerant hardwoods and more than 10 fruit tree species.

Arabuko Farm continues to sell indigenous tree seedlings and grafted fruit tree seedlings (retail & B2B) while serving as a knowledge base for the company’s larger operations.
