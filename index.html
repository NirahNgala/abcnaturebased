Supporting Carbon Developers

Technical and operational partner

Crescendo.green supports carbon developers with the operational capacity needed to implement landscape-based carbon projects.

We focus on reliable propagation systems, planting logistics, field execution, and long-term maintenance to ensure successful project outcomes.

Our experience in plantation systems, agroforestry, and restoration allows us to support a wide range of carbon methodologies across Africa.

Current Work

Dryland Agroforestry & Restoration Project

We are involved in a pilot project focused on dryland agroforestry and landscape restoration.

The project integrates tree planting, assisted natural regeneration, and sustainable land-use systems to improve ecosystem resilience and carbon outcomes.
