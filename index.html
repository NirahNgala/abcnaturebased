Challenges in Producing Quality Planting Material

Low-Quality Seedlings

Poor propagation techniques and weak nursery practices produce seedlings with low survival rates.

Limited Propagation Expertise

Many projects lack the technical knowledge required to propagate indigenous and climate-resilient species.

Shortage of Indigenous Plants

Restoration projects often struggle to source sufficient quantities of native species.

Poor Nursery Management

Inadequate monitoring and nursery practices reduce plant health and overall project success.

What We Deliver

Operational delivery you can outsource

We enables clients to outsource major components of project execution, allowing them to focus on project development, finance, certification and stakeholder engagement.

Seedling Supply and Nursery Operations

We produce and supplies high-quality, dryland-adapted seedlings through its own nurseries. Clients do not need to design, finance or manage nurseries themselves. We deliver planting material that meets project specifications, volumes and timelines.

Planting, Maintenance and Field Management

We manage planting campaigns, early establishment and long-term maintenance using trained field teams, appropriate mechanisation, and site-specific water and protection systems. We take responsibility for survival optimisation across planting seasons and years.

Operational Monitoring and Supervision

Field supervision and operational data collection are integrated into daily operations. ABC manages on-the-ground monitoring to support project management and client reporting requirements.
