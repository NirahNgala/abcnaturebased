El Niño 2026: Why Kenya’s Tree-Planting Sector Should Prepare Now

Climate Systems 8 min read May 2026

Climate agencies are increasingly confident that El Niño conditions are likely to develop in 2026. NOAA’s Climate Prediction Center currently estimates an 82% probability of El Niño emerging during May–July 2026.

For Kenya and the Horn of Africa, this brings real opportunity — especially during the short rains. But rainfall alone is not enough.

Rainfall Creates Opportunity — Preparation Wins

Well-timed rains can dramatically improve seedling survival. However, success depends on having strong, hardened seedlings ready, sites prepared, and teams fully equipped.

🌱 Key Reminder: The best planting seasons are won in the months before the first rain falls.

El Niño Also Brings Risks

Heavy downpours can flood sites, damage roads, increase erosion, and create disease pressure in nurseries. Smart planning turns these risks into manageable challenges.

Why You Should Start Preparing Now

  • Reserve nursery capacity early
  • Secure quality seeds and finalise species lists
  • Plan logistics, transport and field teams
  • Budget for proper aftercare and monitoring
