Our Expertise

Crescendo Green combines large-scale propagation with technical expertise in commercial forestry, ecosystem restoration and agroforestry. This integrated approach enables us to support carbon developers, governments, NGOs and agribusiness investors in delivering resilient, nature-based projects across Africa.

🌳

Commercial Forestry

Building Climate-Resilient Timber Landscapes

Crescendo Green supports the development of sustainable commercial forestry projects with a strong focus on drought-resilient and indigenous tree species suitable for Africa’s drylands.

Our flagship species is Melia volkensii, an exceptional indigenous hardwood that combines excellent timber qualities with strong adaptation to arid and semi-arid environments.

We provide end-to-end support — from species selection and plantation design to planting protocols, water management, and long-term operational strategies.

🌍

Ecosystem Restoration

Restoring Landscapes Through Indigenous Trees

We specialize in the propagation and establishment of indigenous tree species that rebuild ecological function while supporting local communities and climate resilience.

Our practical experience is demonstrated at the Koromi Forestry Project, where over sixty indigenous species have been planted as part of a broader ecosystem recovery effort.

We work closely with partners such as Nature Kenya and Ecosystem Restoration Communities (ERC) to deliver science-based, locally appropriate restoration solutions.

🌱

Agroforestry

Integrating Trees, Agriculture and Climate Solutions

We promote integrated systems that combine commercial trees, indigenous species, and agricultural crops to improve livelihoods while restoring landscapes.

A key focus is the strategic integration of Melia volkensii into farming systems, allowing landowners to diversify income while enhancing resilience.

Our models incorporate cover crops, soil improvement, mulching, and water conservation techniques suitable for Africa’s arid and semi-arid regions.
