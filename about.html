Our Journey

Year Milestone
2019 Development of a 200-hectare agroforestry project in Koromi, Kilifi County.
2022 Expanded operations with a 200-hectare nursery and experimental farm in Kakuyuni.
2024 Selected under JICA's SFS-CORECC programme to produce improved Melia volkensii seedlings.
2026 Launched a large-scale nursery in Mtwapa featuring a 7,500m² shadehouse focused on moringa production for carbon projects.
