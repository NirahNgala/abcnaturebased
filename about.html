Who We Are

Crescendo.green is a Kenya-based operational partner that supports developers, NGOs, investors, and institutions in successfully implementing nature-based projects across Africa.

We allow our partners to outsource the most critical and difficult parts of their projects — large-scale plant propagation, project development support, and hands-on operational delivery in the field.

Our Story

Built on practical, hands-on experience in Kenya, our team has established nurseries, managed large planting operations, and developed resilient agroforestry and forestry systems in challenging dryland environments.

We noticed that many well-designed restoration, agroforestry, and carbon projects struggle or fail during the implementation phase. Crescendo.green was created specifically to close this gap by providing reliable, experienced operational delivery from nursery to landscape.

Our Focus

  • Agroforestry systems that combine trees with crops and livestock
  • Dryland and sub-humid forestry adapted to water-scarce regions
  • Landscape restoration and large-scale reforestation
  • Carbon-aligned land-use and sequestration projects
  • High-quality plant and tree propagation at scale

Our Operations

Mtwapa — Propagation Hub

Large-scale production of high-quality tree seedlings and plants for forestry, agroforestry, and restoration projects.

Arabuko Farm — Experimental Site

Testing drought-resistant crops, plants, and tree species suitable for semi-arid African landscapes.

Koromi Farm — Demonstration Site

Showcasing practical dryland agroforestry and forestry systems, including Melia volkensii plantations and restoration areas.

Our Approach

We are not a consultancy. We are implementers. Everything we do is grounded in real-world experience rather than theory.

Our focus is on achieving higher tree survival rates, stronger long-term growth, and more reliable project outcomes — even in difficult dryland conditions.

Our Impact

  • Significantly improved tree survival and successful landscape restoration
  • Increased carbon sequestration through well-executed projects
  • Creation of sustainable and productive land-use systems
  • Rural employment, skills development, and local capacity building
  • More credible, scalable, and bankable nature-based projects for our partners
