Our Expertise

Crescendo Green combines large-scale propagation with technical expertise in commercial forestry, ecosystem restoration and agroforestry. This integrated approach enables us to support carbon developers, governments, NGOs and agribusiness investors across Africa.

🌳

Commercial Forestry

Building Climate-Resilient Timber Landscapes

We specialize in sustainable commercial forestry using drought-resilient indigenous species, particularly our flagship Melia volkensii.

Crescendo Green supports the development of sustainable commercial forestry projects with a strong focus on drought-resilient and indigenous tree species suitable for Africa's drylands and semi-arid regions.

Our flagship species is Melia volkensii, an exceptional indigenous hardwood that combines excellent timber characteristics with strong adaptation to low rainfall conditions.

We provide end-to-end support — from species selection and plantation design to water management and long-term operational strategies.

Crescendo Green is committed to providing high-quality planting material and technical expertise that enable forestry projects to succeed under challenging conditions across Africa's drylands.

🌍

Ecosystem Restoration

Restoring Landscapes Through Indigenous Trees

We focus on restoring landscapes using diverse indigenous tree species and proven restoration techniques.

Healthy ecosystems depend on diversity. At Crescendo Green, ecosystem restoration is centred on the propagation and establishment of indigenous tree species that rebuild ecological function while supporting local communities and long-term climate resilience.

Our practical experience is demonstrated through restoration activities at the Koromi Forestry Project, where more than sixty indigenous tree species have been planted.

We actively collaborate with Nature Kenya and the Ecosystem Restoration Communities (ERC) network.

🌱

Agroforestry

Integrating Trees, Agriculture and Climate Solutions

We integrate trees with crops to improve livelihoods while restoring land and increasing climate resilience.

Agroforestry offers one of the most effective pathways to improve rural livelihoods while restoring degraded landscapes. We promote integrated systems combining commercial trees, indigenous species and agricultural crops.

A key focus is the strategic integration of Melia volkensii into farming systems along with cover crops, mulching and water conservation techniques.
