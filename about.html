About Us

Our Story

Founded in 2019, our journey began with the development of a 200-hectare agroforestry project in Koromi, Kilifi County.

In 2022, we expanded with a 4-hectare nursery and experimental farm in Kakuyuni.

In 2024, our nursery was selected under JICA’s SFS-CORECC program to produce improved Melia volkensii seedlings.

In 2026, we launched a large-scale nursery in Mtwapa with a 7,500m² shadehouse, currently focused on moringa production for a carbon project.
