Centralised vs Community Nurseries: Finding the Right Model for Scale

When scaling tree planting efforts in Kenya, one of the most important decisions is choosing the right nursery model. Should you go with large centralised nurseries or distributed community-based ones?

Centralised Nurseries: Quality & Efficiency at Scale

Centralised facilities offer better control over genetics, production protocols, hardening processes, and quality assurance. They are ideal for producing hundreds of thousands or millions of high-quality seedlings efficiently.

Community Nurseries: Local Ownership & Employment

Community nurseries create local jobs, build ownership, and reduce transport distances. However, they often struggle with consistency in quality, technical capacity, and economies of scale.

The Hybrid Model: Best of Both Worlds

At Crescendo Green, we advocate for hybrid systems — centralised mother nurseries for elite seedling production combined with well-supported satellite community nurseries. This approach delivers both scale and local benefits.

Key success factors include strong training programmes, consistent technical oversight, shared supply chains for inputs, and clear quality standards across all sites.

“The future of large-scale restoration lies in smart hybrid nursery systems.”
