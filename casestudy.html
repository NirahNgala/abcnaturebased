From Proof of Concept to Scalable Landscape Restoration

Lessons from the Koromi Melia Project

One of the strongest foundations of Crescendo Green is the practical experience gained through our sister company's forestry operations at the Koromi project on the Kenyan coast.

What started as an ambitious dryland forestry initiative has evolved into one of Kenya's largest privately established Melia volkensii plantations and an invaluable learning platform for commercial forestry, ecosystem restoration and carbon project development.

Years of Learning

To date, more than 30,000 Melia volkensii trees have been established alongside over 30,000 additional indigenous trees representing more than 60 species. The project demonstrates that commercial timber production and biodiversity restoration can reinforce each other when designed as an integrated landscape.

Key Lessons Learned

  • Planting holes needed to be significantly larger than originally recommended to promote early root development and drought resilience.
  • Larger nursery containers produced more robust seedlings with improved establishment rates.
  • Timely spot weeding during the first years proved essential to reduce competition for moisture and nutrients.
  • Wind exposure was a critical factor, highlighting the importance of protection measures during the establishment phase.

In parallel, our nursery programme faced the unique challenges of propagating Melia volkensii... Through close collaboration with KEFRI and the JICA-supported SFS-CORECC initiative, we refined our propagation protocols.

Looking Ahead

We are developing a dedicated Melia volkensii seed orchard to strengthen the availability and genetic quality of planting material. Combined with the expansion of our professional propagation capacity in Mtwapa, this positions Crescendo Green to support increasingly ambitious restoration and commercial forestry programmes.

Today, the lessons learned at Koromi underpin our vision for landscape-scale, carbon-ready initiatives that combine commercial forestry, agroforestry and ecosystem restoration.
